# Claims and Limitations
## Supported
- explicit 60/30/7-day renewal windows plus overdue/later/review states;
- visible owner per synthetic record;
- deterministic local date classification from the same inputs;
- CSV export of visible source and computed fields;
- safe missing/invalid-date handling;
- no required runtime network service.
## Not claimed
- production procurement or contract-management readiness;
- legal notice-period compliance;
- financial savings or revenue lift;
- recommendation quality about whether to renew;
- suitability for confidential or regulated data;
- universal fit for every vendor-management process.
